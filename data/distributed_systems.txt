Distributed systems consist of multiple computers working together.

Replication improves fault tolerance.

Partitioning distributes data across multiple machines.

Consensus algorithms maintain consistency.

CAP theorem describes trade-offs between consistency, availability and partition tolerance.

Leader election selects a coordinator node.

Distributed transactions are more complex than local transactions.

Network latency affects system performance.

Eventual consistency is common in distributed databases.

Apache Kafka enables event streaming.
